Where do you use your POS? Pick one. You can use more than one.

  1. In your POS, open the printer settings.
  2. Choose POS Hardware Bridge (it may be called local printer or print bridge).
  3. Copy these two values and paste them into your POS.

Then print a test receipt from your POS. If it doesn’t print, check that the address and password were pasted exactly.
